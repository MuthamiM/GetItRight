How It Works: From Your Vote to Public Proof

From the moment a voter taps their choice to public verification, every step is protected by client-side hardware cryptography, tamper-proof aggregation, and decentralized blockchain anchoring.

01

Hardware Keystore Signing

Voter choices are signed directly within the physical device's Secure Enclave. Private keys never leave the silicon chip, preventing remote ballot tampering at the hardware level.

• Device-Level Security • Tamper-Proof Keys
02

Merkle Leaf Aggregation

Submissions are ingested by high-throughput engines and hashed into cryptographic Merkle leaves. Each voter receives an instant cryptographic receipt proving their vote was counted.

• Append-Only Ledger • Instant Receipts
03

Automated Anomaly Detection

The telemetry pipeline continuously runs automated pattern analysis and turnout velocity checks to instantly flag and freeze suspicious activity at any reporting center.

• Pattern Analysis • Auto-Freeze Protocol
04

Bitcoin Block Anchoring

Periodic Merkle root hashes are committed into public Bitcoin block headers. This creates an unalterable global timestamp that no regime or bad actor can rewrite after the fact.

• Public Blockchain Proof • Immutable Timestamps
