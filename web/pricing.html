Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cryptographic Merkle Checkpoint?

A checkpoint is a signed cryptographic commitment representing thousands of polling station entries compressed into a 32-byte Merkle root. Checkpoints are published regularly and anchored into public blockchains so nobody—not even the platform operators—can alter past records without immediate detection.

What happens if an organization downgrades to a lower tier?

Downgrading never deletes your data. All historical integrity records, Merkle roots, and signed photos remain permanently read-only and verifiable forever by the public.

How do M-Pesa payments work on PollTrack?

We integrate with Safaricom Daraja STK Push. Enter your Kenyan mobile number, confirm the instant prompt on your phone with your M-Pesa PIN, and your subscription or station allocation is activated within seconds.

Can anyone edit or delete a ledger entry?

No. The ledger engine strictly denies all UPDATE and DELETE database operations at the database permission level. Corrections are recorded as new append-only versions that cite the previous sequence number and require a mandatory signed human justification.
