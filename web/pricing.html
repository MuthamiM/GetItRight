Plan questions

What changes between plans?

The same poll, survey, sharing, and results tools are included at every tier. The limits are the poll publishing count and participant target per poll.

How long do paid plans last?

Pro and Organization provide 30 days of access. Renewals are manual.

How does payment activate my plan?

Payment opens iPay's hosted checkout. The server activates the plan only after it verifies the transaction.
