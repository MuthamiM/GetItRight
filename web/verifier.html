How to Verify Completely Independently (Offline / CLI)

You don't have to trust this website. Any observer can verify their result using standard terminal utilities:

# 1. Compute SHA-256 of your captured polling station photo:
sha256sum station_042_form.jpg
> 8f2a93c7e1b4f09d8213ba4e67120cda59e41b7f0129ad8412bc4890cf319a2e

# 2. Fetch inclusion proof from public transparency mirror:
curl -s https://mirror.polltrack.io/v1/proof/8f2a93c7... > proof.json

# 3. Hash leaf with siblings up to root:
python3 -m pt_verify --leaf 8f2a93c7... --proof proof.json
